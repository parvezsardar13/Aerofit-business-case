# AeroFit Treadmill Customer Profiling & Business Analysis

## 📌 Overview
AeroFit is a leading brand in the fitness equipment industry. This project conducts an in-depth Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and conditional probability calculation to identify target customer profiles for AeroFit's three treadmill models:
* **KP281**: Entry-level treadmill 
* **KP481**: Mid-range treadmill 
* **KP781**: Premium-level treadmill 

## 📊 Business Problem
The market research team at AeroFit wants to understand the demographic traits, income levels, and fitness profiles of customers buying each treadmill model to optimize product positioning, target marketing, and customer recommendations.

## 🛠️ Key Analysis Performed
* **Data Preprocessing & Cleaning**: Outlier detection using boxplots and 5th-95th percentile clipping.
* **Univariate & Bivariate Analysis**: Visualized distributions of age, income, miles, and usage across gender and marital status.
* **Probability Analysis**: Marginal and conditional probability matrices to determine purchasing behavior based on demographics.

## 💡 Key Insights
* **Product Popularity**: KP281 leads sales with 44.4%, followed by KP481 (33.3%) and KP781 (22.2%). Budget to mid-range products make up nearly ~78% of total volume.
* **Premium Segment Drivers**: The premium KP781 model is heavily driven by male, high-earning users ($85k+) with high weekly treadmill usage (4–5 days/week).
* **Demographic Reach**: Entry and mid-level models show near gender balance and appeal primarily to young adults (ages 20–35).
